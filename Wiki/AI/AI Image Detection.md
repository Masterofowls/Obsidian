# How AI Image Detection Works

## Overview
AI detection tools identify whether an image was **generated by AI** or created by a human.

## Detection Methods

### Statistical Analysis
- AI images have different **frequency patterns** than real photos
- GANs leave specific artifacts in the frequency domain
- Diffusion models have unique noise patterns

### Artifact Detection
- AI often makes mistakes: extra fingers, inconsistent shadows, blurred backgrounds
- Models trained to recognize these specific artifacts

### Metadata Analysis
- AI generators often embed metadata (C2PA, IPTC)
- Checking for consistency between metadata and image content

### Classifier Models
- Neural networks trained on millions of real vs AI images
- Learn subtle differences invisible to humans

## Limitations
- AI detection is an **arms race** — generators improve alongside detectors
- No method is 100% accurate
- Compressed/re-uploaded images lose detection signals
