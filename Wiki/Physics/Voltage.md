# How Voltage Works

## Overview
Voltage is the **electrical potential difference** between two points — it's the "pressure" that pushes electrons through a circuit.

## Analogy
- Voltage = water pressure in a pipe
- Current = water flow rate
- Resistance = pipe narrowness

## How It Works
- A battery creates voltage by separating charges
- Positive terminal has fewer electrons
- Negative terminal has more electrons
- This difference "pushes" electrons through a circuit

## Units
- Measured in **Volts (V)**
- 1V = 1 Joule per Coulomb
- Common voltages: 1.5V (AA battery), 5V (USB), 120/240V (mains), 12V (car)
