# How Reflection Works

## Overview
Reflection occurs when light bounces off a surface.

## Law of Reflection
- Angle of incidence = Angle of reflection
- Measured from the **normal** (perpendicular to surface)

## Types

### Specular Reflection
- Smooth surface (mirror, calm water)
- Light reflects at a single angle
- Creates clear images

### Diffuse Reflection
- Rough surface (paper, wall)
- Light reflects at many angles
- No clear image, but we can see the surface
