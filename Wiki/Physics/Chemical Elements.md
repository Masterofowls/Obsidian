# What are Chemical Elements

## Overview
A chemical element is a substance that cannot be broken down into simpler substances by chemical means.

## The Periodic Table
- **118 known elements**
- Organized by **atomic number** (number of protons)
- Rows = periods, Columns = groups (similar properties)

## Natural vs Artificial Elements
- **Natural**: Found in nature (hydrogen, carbon, gold — elements 1-94)
- **Artificial**: Created in labs/particle accelerators (elements 95-118)

## How Artificial Elements Are Made
1. **Particle accelerator**: Fire particles at heavy atoms
2. **Nuclear reactor**: Bombard atoms with neutrons
3. **Fusion**: Combine lighter atoms
