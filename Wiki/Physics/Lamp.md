# How Lamps Work

## Incandescent Bulb
- Electric current heats a **tungsten filament** to ~2,500°C
- Hot filament glows (black-body radiation)
- ~90% of energy wasted as heat, only ~10% as light
- Being phased out in many countries

## LED (Light Emitting Diode)
- Electrons flow through a **semiconductor junction**
- Energy released as **photons** (light)
- No heat filament → much more efficient
- Lasts 25-50× longer than incandescent
