# What is an Atom

## Overview
An atom is the **smallest unit of an element** that retains its chemical properties.

## Structure
- **Nucleus** (center): protons + neutrons
  - Protons: positive charge, defines the element
  - Neutrons: no charge, affects isotope
- **Electrons** (orbiting): negative charge
  - Occupy energy levels (shells/orbitals)
  - Determine chemical behavior

## Sizes
- Atom: ~0.1 nanometers (10⁻¹⁰ m)
- Nucleus: ~10⁻¹⁵ m (100,000× smaller than atom)
- If atom were a football stadium, nucleus would be a marble
