# What is Temperature

## Overview
Temperature is a measure of the **average kinetic energy** of particles in a substance.

## How It Works
- Particles (atoms, molecules) are always moving
- Faster movement = higher temperature
- Temperature measures this average motion

## Scales
| Scale | Freezing Point | Boiling Point |
|-------|----------------|---------------|
| Celsius (°C) | 0° | 100° |
| Fahrenheit (°F) | 32° | 212° |
| Kelvin (K) | 0 K (absolute zero) | 373 K |

## Absolute Zero
- 0 Kelvin = -273.15°C = -459.67°F
- Theoretical point where all particle motion stops
- Never actually reached

## Temperature Effects

### On Electricity
- Higher temperature → more resistance in metals (atoms vibrate more → more collisions)
- Superconductors: zero resistance below critical temperature

### On Atmospheric Pressure
- Hot air rises → lower pressure at surface
- Cold air sinks → higher pressure at surface
- This drives weather patterns
