# How Thermal Power Engineering Works

## Overview
Thermal power plants generate electricity by converting **heat energy** into mechanical work, then into electrical energy.

## How It Works
1. Fuel is burned (coal, gas, biomass) or nuclear reaction produces heat
2. Heat boils water → high-pressure steam
3. Steam spins a **turbine** at high speed
4. Turbine drives a **generator** → electricity
5. Steam is cooled and condensed back to water → cycle repeats

## Efficiency
- Typical: 33-45%
- Cogeneration (CHP): up to 80% (uses waste heat for heating)
