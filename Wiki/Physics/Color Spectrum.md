# What is the Color Spectrum

## Overview
The visible color spectrum is the range of **electromagnetic waves** that humans can see.

## Colors (ROYGBIV)
| Color | Wavelength (nm) | Frequency (THz) |
|-------|-----------------|------------------|
| Red | 620-750 | 400-484 |
| Orange | 590-620 | 484-508 |
| Yellow | 570-590 | 508-526 |
| Green | 495-570 | 526-606 |
| Blue | 450-495 | 606-668 |
| Indigo | 425-450 | 668-706 |
| Violet | 380-425 | 706-789 |

## How We See Color
- Objects absorb some wavelengths and reflect others
- Reflected light enters our eyes → cone cells detect RGB
- Brain combines signals → perception of color
