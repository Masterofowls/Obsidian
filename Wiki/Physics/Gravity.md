# How Gravity Works

## Overview
Gravity is a **fundamental force** of attraction between objects with mass.

## Newton's Law
`F = G × (m1 × m2) / r²`
- F = gravitational force
- G = gravitational constant (6.674×10⁻¹¹)
- m1, m2 = masses of the two objects
- r = distance between centers

## Key Facts
- More massive objects → stronger gravity
- Greater distance → weaker gravity (inverse square law)
- Gravity keeps planets in orbit, holds us on Earth
- Einstein's General Relativity: gravity is curvature of spacetime caused by mass
