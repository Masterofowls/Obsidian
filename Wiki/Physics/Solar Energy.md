# How Solar Energy Works

## Overview
Solar energy converts **sunlight into electricity** using photovoltaic (PV) cells.

## How It Works
1. Sunlight hits a **solar panel** made of semiconductor material (usually silicon)
2. Photons knock electrons loose from atoms (**photoelectric effect**)
3. The flow of electrons creates **direct current (DC)**
4. An **inverter** converts DC to **alternating current (AC)** for home use

## Solar Cell Structure
- **N-type layer** (top) — has extra electrons
- **P-type layer** (bottom) — has "holes" (missing electrons)
- **Junction** — where the two layers meet
- Electric field at the junction pushes electrons in one direction

## Efficiency
- Typical panels: 15-22% efficiency
- Theoretical max (single junction): ~33%
- Multi-junction cells: up to 47% (expensive, used in space)
