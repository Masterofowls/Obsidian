# How Translucent Materials Work

## Overview
Materials are classified by how they interact with light:

### Transparent
- Light passes through with minimal scattering
- Clear images visible through the material
- Examples: glass, clear water, air

### Translucent
- Light passes through but is **scattered**
- No clear image visible, but light does get through
- Examples: frosted glass, paper, wax paper, thin fabric

### Opaque
- Light does not pass through at all
- Examples: wood, metal, stone

## How Translucent Works
- Surface irregularities or internal structure scatter light in many directions
- Light gets through but images are blurred/distorted
- Used for: privacy windows, lampshades, diffusers
