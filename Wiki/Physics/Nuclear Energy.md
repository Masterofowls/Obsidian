# How Nuclear Energy Works

## Overview
Nuclear energy is released from the **nucleus of atoms** through nuclear reactions.

## Types of Nuclear Reactions

### Nuclear Fission
- Heavy nucleus splits into lighter nuclei
- Releases energy + neutrons
- Used in: nuclear power plants, atomic bombs
- Fuel: Uranium-235, Plutonium-239

### Nuclear Fusion
- Light nuclei combine to form heavier nucleus
- Releases much more energy than fission
- Used in: the Sun, hydrogen bombs
- Fuel: Hydrogen isotopes (deuterium, tritium)
- Not yet commercially viable for power generation

## How Nuclear Power Plants Work
1. Uranium fuel undergoes fission in the **reactor**
2. Fission releases heat
3. Heat boils water → steam
4. Steam drives a **turbine**
5. Turbine generates electricity

## How Atomic Bombs Work
- **Uncontrolled chain reaction** — too many neutrons released too fast
- Critical mass achieved → massive energy release in microseconds
- Little Boy (Hiroshima): ~15 kilotons
- Fat Man (Nagasaki): ~21 kilotons

## How Atoms Can Be Split
- Neutron bombards uranium nucleus
- Nucleus becomes unstable and splits
- Releases 2-3 neutrons → split more atoms (chain reaction)
