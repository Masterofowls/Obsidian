# How Radiation Works

## Overview
Radiation is the **emission of energy** as waves or particles.

## Types of Radiation

### Electromagnetic (Non-Ionizing)
- Radio waves, microwaves, infrared, visible light, UV
- Does not have enough energy to remove electrons from atoms
- Used for: communication, heating,照明

### Electromagnetic (Ionizing)
- X-rays, gamma rays
- High enough energy to remove electrons → can damage DNA
- Used for: medical imaging, cancer treatment

### Particle Radiation
- Alpha particles (helium nuclei)
- Beta particles (electrons)
- Neutrons
- From radioactive decay

## Electromagnetic Spectrum
| Type | Frequency | Wavelength |
|------|-----------|------------|
| Radio | < 300 MHz | > 1 m |
| Microwave | 300 MHz - 300 GHz | 1 mm - 1 m |
| Infrared | 300 GHz - 400 THz | 750 nm - 1 mm |
| Visible | 400 - 750 THz | 400 - 750 nm |
| UV | 750 THz - 30 PHz | 10 - 400 nm |
| X-ray | 30 PHz - 30 EHz | 0.01 - 10 nm |
| Gamma | > 30 EHz | < 0.01 nm |
