# How Microscopes Work

## Overview
A microscope uses **lenses** to magnify objects too small to see with the naked eye.

## Optical Microscope

### How It Works
1. Light passes through (or reflects off) the specimen
2. **Objective lens** collects light and creates a magnified image
3. **Eyepiece lens** further magnifies the image
4. Total magnification = objective × eyepiece (e.g., 40×10 = 400×)

### Resolution
- Limited by **diffraction of light** (~200nm)
- Can see cells, bacteria, but not viruses or atoms

## Electron Microscope
- Uses **electron beams** instead of light
- Much higher resolution (~0.1nm)
- Can see viruses, proteins, even atoms
- Two types: SEM (surface), TEM (cross-section)
