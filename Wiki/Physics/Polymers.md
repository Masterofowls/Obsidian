# What are Polymers

## Overview
Polymers are large molecules made of **repeating units** (monomers) linked together.

## Examples
| Polymer | Monomer | Use |
|---------|---------|-----|
| Polyethylene | Ethylene | Plastic bags, bottles |
| PVC | Vinyl chloride | Pipes, insulation |
| Nylon | Caprolactam | Clothing, rope |
| DNA | Nucleotides | Genetic code |

## Natural Polymers
- DNA, RNA, proteins, cellulose, rubber
- Made by living organisms

## Synthetic Polymers
- Plastics, synthetic fibers, adhesives
- Made by humans in labs/factories
