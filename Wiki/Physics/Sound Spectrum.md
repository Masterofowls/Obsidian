# What is Sound Wave Spectrum

## Overview
Sound is a **mechanical wave** that travels through air (or other media) by compressing and expanding molecules.

## How It Works
1. Source vibrates (vocal cords, speaker cone)
2. Vibrations compress and expand surrounding air
3. Pressure waves travel outward at ~343 m/s (speed of sound in air)
4. Ear detects pressure changes → brain interprets as sound

## Frequency Spectrum
| Range | Type |
|-------|------|
| 20-20,000 Hz | Human hearing range |
| < 20 Hz | Infrasound (elephants, earthquakes) |
| > 20,000 Hz | Ultrasound (bats, medical imaging) |
