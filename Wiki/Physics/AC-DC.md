# What is the Difference Between DC and AC

## DC (Direct Current)
- Current flows in **one direction**
- Constant voltage
- Sources: batteries, solar panels, USB chargers
- Used in: electronics, LED lights, EVs

## AC (Alternating Current)
- Current **alternates direction** periodically
- Voltage rises and falls in a sine wave
- Sources: power plants, wall outlets
- Used in: household wiring, industrial equipment

## Why AC for Power Distribution
- AC can be easily **transformed** to different voltages
- High voltage = low current = less energy loss in transmission
- Step-up transformers for transmission, step-down for homes

## Frequency
- US: 60 Hz (direction changes 120 times/second)
- Europe/Asia: 50 Hz
