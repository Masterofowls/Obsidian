# How Electric Resistance Works

## Overview
Resistance is how much a material **opposes the flow** of electric current.

## How It Works
- Electrons flowing through a material collide with atoms
- These collisions convert electrical energy into **heat**
- More collisions = more resistance

## Factors Affecting Resistance
| Factor | Effect |
|--------|--------|
| Material | Copper (low) vs rubber (high) |
| Length | Longer = more resistance |
| Thickness | Thinner = more resistance |
| Temperature | Hotter = more resistance (for metals) |

## Ohm's Law
`R = V / I`
- Resistance = Voltage / Current

## Superconductors
- At very low temperatures, some materials have **zero resistance**
- Current flows forever without energy loss
- Used in: MRI machines, particle accelerators, quantum computers
