# What is Electric Parts Frequency

## Overview
The frequency of electronic components determines **how fast** they can operate.

## What It Affects
- **CPU/GPU**: Clock speed (GHz) determines instructions per second
- **RAM**: DDR frequency (e.g., DDR5-6400 = 6400 MHz)
- **Display**: Refresh rate (60Hz, 144Hz) = frames per second
- **Storage**: SSD controller speed

## How Frequency Affects Performance
- Higher frequency → more operations per second
- But: higher frequency → more heat → more power consumption
- Trade-off between speed and energy efficiency
