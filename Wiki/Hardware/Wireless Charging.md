# How Wireless Charging Works

## Overview
Wireless charging uses **electromagnetic induction** to transfer energy between two coils without wires.

## How It Works
1. **Transmitter coil** (in the charger) — AC current creates a **changing magnetic field**
2. **Receiver coil** (in the phone/device) — The changing magnetic field induces a voltage in the coil
3. **Rectification** — The induced AC is converted to DC
4. **Regulation** — Voltage is regulated to charge the battery

## Qi Standard
- Most common wireless charging standard
- Uses 110-205 kHz frequency
- Efficiency: ~60-80% (vs ~95% for wired)
- Charges at 5-15W typically

## Limitations
- Energy loss as heat (lower efficiency)
- Must be aligned properly
- Short range (a few mm)
- Cannot charge through thick cases
