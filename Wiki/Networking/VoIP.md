# How VoIP Works

## Overview
VoIP (Voice over Internet Protocol) allows **voice calls over the internet** instead of traditional phone lines.

## How It Works
1. Microphone captures your voice → converts to digital data
2. Data is compressed and packetized
3. Packets travel over the internet
4. Packets are decompressed and played through speaker on other end

## Protocols
- **SIP** — Session Initiation Protocol (sets up/tears down calls)
- **RTP** — Real-time Transport Protocol (carries audio/video)
- **SRTP** — Secure RTP (encrypted)

## Examples
- Skype, Zoom, Discord, WhatsApp calls
- Landline VoIP phones (office phones)
