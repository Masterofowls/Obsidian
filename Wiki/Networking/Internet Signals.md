# How Wires Transfer Internet Signal Instantly

## Overview
Internet data travels through **fiber optic cables** (light) and **copper cables** (electrical signals) at near the speed of light.

## Speed of Transmission
- **Fiber optic**: Light travels at ~200,000 km/s in glass (~2/3 speed of light)
- **Copper**: Electrical signals travel at ~2/3 speed of light
- For reference: New York to London (~5,500 km) takes ~27ms via fiber

## Fiber Optic
- Pulses of **laser light** travel through thin glass fibers
- Total internal reflection keeps light bouncing inside the fiber
- Single-mode fiber: long distance, high speed
- Multi-mode fiber: shorter distance, cheaper

## Submarine Cables
- ~500 submarine cables carry 99% of intercontinental data
- Can be 10,000+ km long
- Spaced ~50-100km apart with repeaters

## Not Instant
- Speed of light in fiber: ~200,000 km/s
- 1ms = 200km travel
- Real-world latency is due to: propagation delay + routing + processing
