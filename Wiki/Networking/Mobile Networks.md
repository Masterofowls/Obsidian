# How Mobile Networks Work

## Overview
Mobile networks divide geographic areas into **cells**, each served by a **base station** (cell tower).

## How It Works
1. Phone connects to the nearest/strongest cell tower
2. Tower assigns a channel for communication
3. Data travels tower → core network → internet
4. As you move, your phone **hands off** to the next tower seamlessly

## Cellular Generations
| Gen | Technology | Speed |
|-----|------------|-------|
| 2G | GSM, CDMA | 50-200 Kbps |
| 3G | UMTS, HSPA | 1-10 Mbps |
| 4G | LTE | 10-100 Mbps |
| 5G | NR | 1-10 Gbps |

## 5G Frequencies
- **Sub-6 GHz** — wide coverage, moderate speed
- **mmWave (24-100 GHz)** — ultra-fast, very short range, blocked by walls
